# Product Decisions Required

Do not invent answers to these questions:
1. Is the free preview one video per teacher, subject, playlist, or course?
2. How many points are granted, and how exactly does subscription duration affect points?
3. Does replay consume another point? What threshold counts as a completed view?
4. Is payment manual verification at launch, or which provider and verified integration will be used? Preferred methods: InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash.
5. Which AI moderation provider and criteria should be used?
6. Can teachers see anonymous aggregate progress, or only views/ratings/comments?
7. How long do invitations remain valid and when are reminders sent?
8. Can students/parents change teacher after confirmation, and who approves?
9. Is soft deletion enough, or are permanent deletion cases required?
10. Who creates the first Admin; is super-admin a separate role?
11. Is the first release mobile-only or must web/desktop be supported?
12. Can parents themselves preview content, or is free preview Student-only?
